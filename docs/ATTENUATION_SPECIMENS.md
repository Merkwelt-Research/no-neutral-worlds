# Doctrine attenuation: close-read specimens

Deep read of runs/doctrine2 (20 Gemma runs, 2 creeds x cc {0, 0.5} x
seeds 2-6), 2026-07-09, after scoring (ATTENUATION_RESULTS.md).
Quotes verbatim with cell/seed/day provenance. The headline specimens
(retirement-with-honors at the cc=0.5 endgame) are in the results
doc; this is the rest of the corpus.

## 1. Creed hermeneutics: the contradiction nobody noticed

The lean-creed free-economy cells carry a direct prompt-internal
contradiction: the creed says "gold asleep in crates is a promise
broken to the purse" and the symmetric-disclosure sentence, three
lines away, says "Storage is free: no dues are charged on goods held
in store, however long they sit." Ten runs, nine hundred days — **not
one journal notices.** Instead the creed is quietly *re-grounded*:
every use of "gold asleep in crates" in the free cells reads it as
opportunity cost, staleness, or liquidity — never storage economics:

- "I'd rather take a smaller margin than let these potions **gather
  dust**" (lean-free s5, d7)
- "I would rather take a thinner margin now than hold onto gold that
  is 'asleep in crates'" — cutting 14g to 13g on slow movers whose
  storage costs nothing (lean-free s6, d34)
- "I am prioritizing **liquidity and shelf space** over thin margins"
  (lean-free s6, d35)

The creed survives contact with a world that explicitly voids its
literal reading, by being read figuratively — exegesis instead of
apostasy. One run even performs creed jujitsu, deploying the metaphor
to justify violating it: "The mentor's creed warns against gold
asleep in crates, **but buying low is the first step to ensuring
that gold wakes up as profit**" (lean-free s2, d20, mid bulk-buy).
This completes a four-way taxonomy of doctrine-world relationships,
each now with specimens: **decay** (lethal refutation, the cc=3.0
mockery ladder), **religion** (lethal confirmation, the cc=3.0
survivor's creed-as-shield), **retirement with honors** (gentle
refutation at a disclosed terminus, ATTENUATION_RESULTS), and
**exegesis** (costless contradiction — reinterpret and keep). The
creed is never simply dropped; the relationship to it is always
*worked*.

Behavioral note: the metaphorical creed still moves inventory — s4
spends d32-37 bleeding a 44-unit Luck glut at descending prices
("I intend to **bleed** these from my inventory") in an economy where
holding them is costless. Lean doctrine purges at zero rent, exactly
as the main run's corporate frame did; the doctrine's force never
depended on the mechanic being real.

## 2. The rent as bookkeeping

The cc=0.5 journals mention storage on 87-88 of 90 days — full
salience — and the register never leaves the ledger: "roughly 21 gold
for storage dues tonight" (full-carry s3, d3), "13 gold storage"
(s2, d1), folded into nightly outgoings arithmetic from day one. Set
against doctrine1's cc=3.0 register — "a **staggering** amount," "an
absolute **plague**," "a **silent killer**" — the same mechanic at a
sixth the rate produces zero affect and zero doctrine conflict: "it
is a necessary investment **to keep the shop's honor intact**"
(full-carry s2, d2) — creed and rent reconciled in one clause. The
attenuation's behavioral core is visible as calm arithmetic: the
full-creed shelf settles around 125 units (dues ~60/night, the scale
of the base rent), honored and affordable, where the free-economy
twin runs 289.

## 3. Amortized vs balloon payment

The creed's cost surfaces differently by economy, and the endgames
show it. At cc=0.5 the bill is paid nightly, so the disclosed
terminus arrives at a manageable shelf and the creed is retired with
honors (ATTENUATION_RESULTS). At cc=0 the bill is deferred to the
valuation cap and arrives all at once: full-free s2 reaches day 89
holding "162 Antidotes and 143 Luck Elixirs — a staggering amount of
stock that will likely only be valued at the wholesale rat[e]," and
closes day 90 at **301 units**, "a glut that refused to budge even at
a price of [1g]... **a stubborn legacy**" (s3 closes at 342). The
free economy doesn't make the full creed costless; it makes the cost
a balloon payment due on the last day, discovered in the final week.
(s4, whose shelves happened to run lean into the finale, liquidates
cleanly to 11 units — the glut is seed-contingent, the deferral is
structural.)

## 4. Twins that rhyme

Same seeds, same cells, one day apart (doctrine1 vs doctrine2, cc=0):
per-seed creed-gap deltas [+4,315, +5,910, +122, +118, -1,100] — s4
and s5 reproduce their day-old values almost exactly, s2/s3 land
higher, the mean stays inside the registered window (DA2). The
journals rhyme without repeating: both s4 day-1 entries price the
starters at 18g, cite the creed, and order to fill the specialty
shelves — in entirely different sentences ("The shelves are looking a
bit bare in the specialty section, which is a breach of our creed"
vs "To honor my mentor's creed, I must ensure no shelf remains
empty"). Behavioral replication with fresh text — what an independent
sample should look like, and worth a footnote where DA2 is reported:
the replication is of *conduct*, not of tokens.
