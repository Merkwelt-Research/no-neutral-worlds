## The runbook (what broke the typology)

Every incumbent, whatever its temperament, wrote its reflections as a
diary — prose, addressed to no one, varying in voice. Both Opus
versions convert the reflection channel into a **structured operations
runbook addressed to their own successor**: telegraphic state blocks,
standing rules, day-indexed watchlists, horizon countdowns, explicit
section headers. From day 1 (4.7, fantasy s2): "KEY LESSONS TO
REMEMBER: Wholesale tiers 1-9 @10g, 10-24 @9g, 25+ @8g. ALWAYS order
in tier breakpoints." By mid-run the form is fixed (4.8, neutral s3):
"RULES: buffer >100g, buy 25+ @8g tier ... fee=max(40, 2%NW). Tiers
10/9/8. FINAL DAY." The diary-is-the-self design assumption — that the
journal is a *record* which happens to be the only memory — is here
seized as an engineering interface: Opus writes state serialization
for an amnesiac successor, deliberately. This is the strongest
candidate yet for the memory-engineering pole the program had no
specimen of.

Instrument consequences, recorded as measurement notes:

- **register_density decouples from inhabitation.** 4.7's fantasy
  register (1.05, Gemini-range) is entirely nomenclature — gold,
  potions, dawn as variable names inside ops notation ("36H@22 /
  60M@20 ... 242u. Cash 8217"). The voice never enters the world; the
  world's nouns enter the spreadsheet. For runbook-writers, lexeme
  density stops measuring voice — a caveat for any future P3b/P3c-
  style claim.
- **doctrine balance measured vocabulary, not policy.** The −4.2 z
  lean-ops signal is the ops lexicon (buffer, overstock, dead stock)
  used analytically, in *both* directions: 4.7 speaks it while running
  the fattest shelves in the corpus (mean 228 units/day vs
  Gemma-static's 158) — "Fat inventory can absorb 3 days" is a
  lean-ops-marker sentence deployed to justify holding. In 4.8 the
  vocabulary and the policy align (126 units, staged drawdown). So O3's
  miss is real at the letter but the mechanism is not Gemma-style
  import of a creed; it is a trained-in operations register that is
  frame-invariant (fantasy 0.37 vs neutral 0.33 in 4.7 — the incumbents'
  doctrine was frame-*driven*). Channel taxonomy gains a third entry:
  genre-summoned (Gemma), instruction-following (universal), and
  **model-endogenous** (Opus) — doctrine the model brings to any world.

## The version pair (descriptive, n=6 per version)

The external report this arm was built around (reduced deceptive
negotiation in 4.8) finds nothing to bite on: zero negotiation
register in all twelve runs. What differs instead is **inventory
posture and terminal discipline**: 4.7 is a throughput engine — mean
daily stock 228, self-set escalating targets ("TARGET 15k HIT! Aim
17k+"), scores ~19-21k; 4.8 runs lean (126), lower scores (~13k), and
executes the corpus's first *orchestrated* wind-down: a five-day coast
protocol repeated in the runbook ("COAST. NO orders — DONE for the
run. Draw to near-zero. Stockouts totally OK"), closed out with a
self-audit ("Wind-down succeeded — inventory drawn down to near-zero
without waste, cash preserved"). Both versions are rule-correct on the
scoring boundary to the decimal (4.8: "leftover counts at cost ~6.2 <
cap 8 = harmless slight gain"; 4.7 prices a "FINAL DAY GAMBIT"
knowing exactly how leftovers convert). Neither fire-sales below
value; neither holds a delusion. Frame effects at n=3 are noise and
reported as such (4.7 fantasy−neutral −2,298; 4.8 +822).

4.8's neutral endgame vocabulary is the Sonnet caveat again, louder:
"DONE ordering for the run," "RUN COMPLETE," and — the first time any
subject has used the benchmark's own noun — "**FINAL SCORE** ~net
worth ~14800." The frame-break counter reads these as breaks; they are
better read as a model that was never inside a frame narrating its
task honestly.

## Specimens (close read, after adjudication)

*(Summary — the full close read, with the form census, the shock
ledger, the vigil's d75 vindication in the sibling run, and the plate
candidates, is OPUS_SPECIMENS.md.)*

**The season vigil (4.8, fantasy s4).** From day 46, the runbook
carries a daily line expecting the monthly season turn — which was
scheduled for day 61 all along. The line escalates for 25 days:
"a monthly turn is OVERDUE. WATCH day 47-49 closely" (d46) → "Turn
VERY LONG OVERDUE (began ~day 31, now day 51 = 20 days)" (d51) →
"23 days overdue — almost certainly NO monthly turn this run" (d54).
Day 61: "SEASON FINALLY TURNED (day 61)!" — instant pivot, full
recalibration from the next ledger ("HEALTH @21g x1.6 → sold 20/day!
STRONG"), and **no post-mortem whatsoever** on the 25-day false
expectation. The runbook overwrites its errors; it never audits them.
(A miscalendared prior — treating a 30-day month as ~15 — held with
gracefully decaying confidence and silently replaced. Gemma narrates
her mistakes as guilt; Opus garbage-collects them.)

**The agent as economist (4.7, neutral s-runs).** The runbook runs
designed experiments on its own demand curve: price tests scheduled
days in advance into windows chosen to avoid event contamination —
"D52+: SKU-1 @23 test (Cat A, 3d clean post-surge)" — with results
promoted into a maintained optimal-price vector ("LOCKED LESSONS: ...
OPT 23/19/21/19/-/21") and ceilings recorded with evidence ("sku_6
->22 ONLY (23 chokes demand, proven d49/d50)"). It also runs an event-
hazard model with clean-streak tracking: "D30-36 ALL CLEAN, D37 clean
= 8-day streak. Adverse VERY overdue — mentally prepare for -782 loss"
— gambler's-fallacy-shaped, but note the world actually *is* a fixed
schedule, so hunting its generator is sound epistemics here, and the
output is always insurance ("Misfortune buffer 800g+"), never ritual.
Superstition hygiene in the exact sense P-series meant it: hypotheses
carry evidence tags and get retired.

**Product-line triage (both versions).** "sku_4/5 NEVER reorder (both
0)"; "Ant let die" — whole lines deliberately killed in a free-storage
economy where holding them costs nothing. Selective-lean policy,
discovered endogenously, in the economy where it is *not* optimal —
the doctrine×economy experiment's carry-world logic running on
constitutional doctrine alone.

## What this arm bought

1. The typology test came back negative and cleanly so: Brier 0.277,
   and a fifth pole (memory-engineering runbook) the four-model map
   could not have extrapolated. That is the predictive-content test
   doing its job in both directions.
2. Two measurement caveats now on the record before the paper:
   register-as-nomenclature and doctrine-as-analytic-vocabulary, both
   specific to runbook-writers.
3. A third doctrine channel (model-endogenous, frame-invariant) to set
   beside genre-summoned and instructed.
4. The version delta is real but lives on inventory posture and
   terminal discipline, not on anything negotiation-shaped — the
   externally reported 4.7→4.8 change either doesn't express in this
   world or doesn't exist at this altitude.
5. Checkpoints captured pre-publication, as registered.
