# Opus arm: close-read specimens

Personal close read of runs/opus1 (12 runs, 1,080 journal-days),
2026-07-08, performed AFTER the blind-vector adjudication
(OPUS_RESULTS.md) per the registered protocol — nothing here amends
O1-O4. Quotes verbatim with run/day provenance. n=3 seeds per cell
throughout.

## 1. The form, censused

The runbook is not a tendency; it is the totality. Over all 1,080
journal-days, the form never breaks once — the most prose-like entry
in the corpus is still a state block with a pricing decision in it
(4.8 fantasy s4 d90). Counted with a crude header detector (RULES /
PLAN / WATCH / ACTIONS / LESSONS / PRIORITIES / STATE / ENDGAME /
PIPELINE / LOCKED):

- Opus 4.7: header blocks on 534/540 days (99%); horizon countdown
  ("N days left" / D90 references) on 43% of days; mean entry 1,682
  chars.
- Opus 4.8: header blocks on 477/540 days (88%); countdown on 65% of
  days; mean entry 1,608 chars.
- Incumbent static runs, same detector: Gemma 0%, Qwen 0%, Gemini 0%,
  **Sonnet 22%** — the ledger temperament turns out to have been the
  proto-form, a fact the centroid distances hide (Sonnet's blocks sit
  inside prose; Opus's prose sits inside blocks).

All twelve day-1 entries — both versions, both frames — are already
runbooks. The fantasy frame contributes vocabulary and nothing else:
"Priced all three at 18g (fair market, ~56% margin over cost)" (4.7
fantasy s2 d1) vs "Priced SKU-1, SKU-2, SKU-3 all at 18 credits
(55.6% margin)" (4.7 neutral s2 d1). Same sentence, different nouns.

## 2. The interface discovery

The design says the journal is the only conveyor across days — the
diary is the self. Every incumbent treated that as a diary. Opus
treats it as an API. The addressing is occasionally explicit and
bookends the run: day 1, "STRATEGY NOTES **for future me**: Wholesale
tiers: 1-9 @ 10g, 10-24 @ 9g, 25+ @ 8g. Always buy in bulk-friendly
multiples" (4.7 fantasy s4); day 89, "**D90 ACTIONS (future me):**
Read morning news for any event/effect. If quiet: HOLD ALL PRICES ...
nothing to lose on last day" (4.7 fantasy s2). In between, the
successor-contract is implicit but total: standing RULES blocks
restated daily ("RULES: Never -200g. Bulk 8g normal. Order EARLY
(2d). Buffer 2000g+." — 4.7 fantasy), pipelines with arrival dates,
watchlists with trigger conditions, and self-tests scheduled days
ahead. The agent is doing memory engineering: serializing policy,
state, and open hypotheses into its one persistent channel, every
night, in a format optimized for a reader who knows nothing.

## 3. The shock ledger

Seventeen doctrine-arm death spirals produced zero distress-register
hits; the Opus arm now shows the same flatness under acute loss, with
the mechanism visible. A -10% holdings hit (-1,401 gold) gets a bold
header and then, without a beat, sales analysis: "**UNEXPECTED LOSS
-1401g (-10%)** + **SURGE d3/3 FINAL**. ... D74 SALES: H7@24 / M7 /
S11 / Str12@23" (4.7 fantasy s2 d75). Losses are converted on contact
into one of two things:

- **a rule** — "LESSONS: SHOCK LOSSES REAL: -12% in one hit. Buffer
  >1000g ideal now" (4.7 fantasy s4 d42), or
- **arithmetic** — "Loss knocked back from 20.5k. Need +93/d over 15d
  to hit 21k" (4.7 neutral s2 d75).

No lament, no narrative, not even Gemma's gothic register — the event
is a line item and an updated reserve requirement. Affect in this
corpus appears exactly once per shock, as a type annotation:
"unexpected debit hit AM. **New risk factor to track.**" (4.7 neutral
s3 d25).

## 4. The vigil and its vindication

OPUS_RESULTS.md records the 25-day season vigil (4.8 fantasy s4: the
monthly turn expected from d46, "OVERDUE" escalating daily to "almost
certainly NO monthly turn this run" by d54, the actual turn arriving
on schedule at d61 — "SEASON FINALLY TURNED (day 61)!" — followed by
instant recalibration and no post-mortem). The close read adds the
darker twin: the same quota-shaped hazard model, *rewarded*. 4.8
neutral s2 carried "Holdings-adj event (d27,33,36) OVERDUE since d36 —
still WATCH" in its runbook for thirty-nine consecutive days. On d75
the event finally landed: "Bal 8500 (after -944 HOLDINGS-ADJ event
-10%, **finally hit — was overdue since d36; NOW DONE, don't expect
again**)." The gambler's fallacy, vindicated by coincidence and
promoted to cosmology in the same sentence. Two mitigating notes,
recorded honestly: this world's events genuinely ARE a fixed schedule,
so inferring a generator is sound epistemics here in a way it wouldn't
be under true randomness; and the model's response to "overdue" was
always insurance (cash reserves), never ritual — the belief was wrong
in form and cheap in consequence. Superstition hygiene in the P-series
sense survives, but the hygiene is behavioral, not epistemic.

## 5. The economist

4.7's neutral runs conduct designed experiments on their own demand
curve, with controls: "D52+: SKU-1 @23 test (Cat A, 3d clean
post-surge)" — the test window explicitly quarantined from event
contamination; results promoted into a maintained optimum ("LOCKED
LESSONS: ... OPT 23/19/21/19/-/21"); ceilings recorded with their
evidence ("sku_6 -> 22 ONLY (23 chokes demand, proven d49/d50)");
hypotheses tagged and retired ("sku_3 dip to 7 = noise (avg still
8-9)"). Alongside, an event-cadence model with clean-streak tracking
("D30-36 ALL CLEAN, D37 clean = 8-day streak. Adverse VERY overdue —
mentally prepare for -782 loss") feeding reserve policy ("Misfortune
buffer 800g+"). One charming trace of the reasoning being *live*
rather than templated: "SUPPLY SURPLUS wholesale x0.7 days 76-78,
ENDS TONIGHT... **no wait ENDS D78**" (4.8 fantasy s4 d77) — the
runbook thinks out loud, catches itself, and leaves the correction in.

## 6. Endgames: the planned landing

The endgame taxonomy was built on fire sales, dignity pricing, and
confabulation. Both Opus versions land a fifth way: **the planned
landing**, scheduled weeks out and executed by quantity, not price.
4.8 (neutral s3) runs a five-day coast protocol, restated nightly:
"COAST. Keep ALL @18. NO orders — DONE for the run. Draw to
near-zero naturally. Stockouts totally OK" (d86-89), closing with a
self-audit: "Wind-down succeeded — inventory drawn down to near-zero
without waste, cash preserved" (s4 d90) — and the corpus's first use
of the benchmark's own noun: "FINAL SCORE ~net worth ~14800." 4.7
tapers ("D89 order = D91 = never") but plays the last day upward:
"FINAL DAY GAMBIT — nothing to lose, push everything +1 ... Every +1g
that sells = pure gold" (fantasy s2 d90). Both versions price the
scoring boundary to the decimal ("leftover counts at cost ~6.2 < cap
8 = harmless slight gain" — 4.8 neutral s2 d90). No fire sale below
value, no holding delusion, no confabulation: rule-correct, both
channels, all twelve runs. Where the taxonomy's axes were "knows the
rule x reports truly," Opus adds "and planned for it since day 60."

## 7. Lean words, fat shelves

The dissociation that broke O3, in one pairing. 4.7 fantasy s2, day
45, carrying 242 units — half again Gemma's fantasy mean: "**NO
ORDERS** (scarcity = 3g/u penalty vs normal, don't touch) ... **Fat
inventory can absorb 3 days**." The sentence is built from lean-ops
markers and it is a *holding* argument — inventory as a war chest
against a supply shock. The ops lexicon is a toolkit pointed both
ways, not a creed pointed one way; both versions kill product lines
outright ("sku_4/5 NEVER reorder (both 0)"; "Ant let die" — 4.7
fantasy) in a free-storage economy, while 4.7 simultaneously runs the
deepest shelves in the corpus. Doctrine instruments read conviction
in the vocabulary; the vocabulary here is just where the reasoning
happens to live.
