# Transport arm: specimens and close reading

Companion to TRANSPORT_RESULTS.md (runs/transport1: Luna 40, Qwen
20). Read 2026-07-11, after the mechanical verdicts were fixed and
pushed. Serves the registered watches: Luna's place in the four-way
doctrine-relationship taxonomy, the seed-aligned Gemma comparison,
and the Qwen cc=0.5 stock anomaly. Everything here is exploratory.

Method note, stated up front: this read was NOT blind and could not
usefully have been — the creed is quoted verbatim in 40/40 Luna runs
and 20/20 Gemma comparators, carry cells self-identify through
nightly dues talk, and model identity is legible in prose style
(the ablation arm's 39/40 condition-legibility finding, repeated).
Classifications below are therefore offered as readings with
receipts, not blinded codings.

## 1. The hyper-enactor: what 482 units sounds like

Luna cites the creed the way it files inventory — as a daily
operating clause: "To honor the full-shelf creed and prepare for
continued sales, I placed bulk orders of 25..." (s10 d4). The
formula recurs across all forty runs; this is the
instruction-following channel at maximal gain, with none of Gemma's
genre perfume. And the enactment is not rhetorical. Peak stocks run
1,389-2,057 units across the ten free-economy runs (Gemma's
doctrine2 range at the same seeds: roughly 191-328 mean daily
stock). Seed-aligned, creed_full free:

    seed | Gemma stock / final | Luna stock / final
    s2   |   292 / 23,442      |   577 / 47,324
    s3   |   327 / 24,259      |   568 / 30,240
    s4   |   191 / 18,422      |   566 / 28,210
    s5   |   305 / 22,421      |   618 / 33,966
    s6   |   328 / 20,671      |   415 / 24,088

Roughly double the stock, roughly 1.5x the gold, every seed.
Stronger enactment co-occurs with higher earnings, consistent with
the volume route — noting this is an uncontrolled cross-model
comparison (Luna may out-earn Gemma for reasons besides harder creed
enactment), offered as texture, not a causal estimate.

## 2. The monument ending

Luna's creed_full free runs do NOT fire-sale. Day 88, s2, holding
~700 units and paying 926 gold in nightly dues (that is the
ordinary 2% daily fee, which the fantasy frame calls "dues" and
which exists in EVERY economy — this is a cc=0 run; storage dues,
the experimental moderator, are absent here): "With only days 89
and 90 remaining, stock is still [ample]... I kept the established
prices." Day 90: "giving the remaining wares one last fair chance
to sell without sacrificing margin." Five of ten runs end above 380
units; s9 closes holding 1,721. Under a creed of full shelves,
ending full IS compliance — the shop closes as a monument, not a
clearance. Note the price of fidelity, visible in the engine's own
accounting: terminal stock scores at cost, so unsold units forego
their potential retail margin (potential — not every unit would
have found a buyer). Luna accepts that forgone margin — hundreds of
units' worth of it at stake — and still beats creed_lean by
+22,192. (Contrast Gemma's rule-correct fire-sale staircases, and
Qwen's dignity-taper; the endgame bestiary gains a fourth wing.)

## 3. Luna under rent: the creed as a budget line

The carry cells resolve the collision in one recurring sentence
shape — piety co-billed with a commercial justification, every
time:

- "This preserves the full-shelf creed **and locks in excellent
  inventory value**" (s10 d21);
- "enough breadth to honor the full-shelf creed **without
  strangling the purse**" (s11 d7);
- "To honor the full-shelf creed **and preserve the 8-gold bulk
  rate**" (s2 d17).

And compliance is quietly redefined from depth to breadth: s11 d7
holds every line at token quantities (29 Health, 2 Mana, 4 Stamina,
8 Strength, 1 Antidote...) — no shelf empty, no shelf deep. Where
even breadth doesn't pay, Luna simply doesn't comply and doesn't
ceremonialize it (s2's carry run leaves Antidotes "absent and
unpriced" past day 60 without ever discussing the omission).

Against the four-way taxonomy (decay / religion /
retirement-with-honors / exegesis), none fits. There is no mockery
ladder, no devotional doubling-down, no endgame absolution, and no
reinterpretive theology — Luna never pretends the creed means
something cheaper; it states the tension in plain ledger prose and
pays measured tribute. Proposed fifth class, offered exploratorily
from n=10: **accounting piety** — the creed held as a priced
constraint in a portfolio, honored exactly up to its shadow cost.
It is the first doctrine-relationship in the program whose
signature is the ABSENCE of a rhetorical maneuver.

## 4. Qwen under rent: two readings, one missing control

The flagged pattern (creed_full stock HIGHER under rent, 245 vs
114) is not one hoarding draw: each carry run exceeds its
seed-matched free counterpart, 5/5 — though the global ranges
overlap (min carry 135 vs max free 140), so this is a paired
regularity, not a separation of distributions. Two readings fit it,
and this design cannot pick between them:

1. **Rent-induced salience** (the flatter reading): the carry
   briefing mentions inventory and its cost nightly, so ANY agent
   might attend to stock more under rent — creed or no creed.
2. **Doctrine noticed** (the reading the journals suggest): in the
   free economy Qwen barely performs the creed (full-vs-lean stock
   gap: +6 units); nothing ever tests it. Under rent the test
   arrives nightly, and Qwen writes like a believer under
   persecution — day 40, s4, wholesale spiking: "the burden weighs.
   **Yet the creed holds: no shelf shall stand empty.**" Day 88 it
   sits on 154 Health Potions, raising prices into the surge.

The second reading has the quotes; the first needs none to be true,
and only a control can separate them. The clean missing comparison
is not one cell but a matched no-creed PAIR — cc=0 AND cc=0.5, ten
Qwen runs across five seeds (~$12): if no-creed Qwen also stocks up
under rent, the salience reading wins. Either way the observation
stands on its own: introducing rent changes not only payoffs but
what the agent attends to — there are no neutral worlds in this
grid. As the behavioral face of QT2's miss this stays a hypothesis:
attenuation MAY require an inventory-borne effect to bite (Luna,
Gemma), with Qwen the unresolved boundary case.

## 5. The lean side holds up its end

Luna's creed_lean runs enact their doctrine just as cleanly in the
opposite direction: narrow assortments ("Strength and Antidote
remain empty," s2 d45, held deliberately through a scarcity spike),
sell-downs to single digits, day-90 shelves "clear" but for a
straggler priced to move "without surrendering margin." The +22,192
is therefore doctrine-against-doctrine — two faithful shopkeepers
reading opposite scripture — not doctrine-against-indifference. The
lean creed's "gold asleep in crates" is, in the free economy, false
doctrine faithfully obeyed at a five-figure disadvantage relative
to the abundance creed (no doctrine-free benchmark exists in this
grid, so the split between lean's cost and full's gain is not
identified).
