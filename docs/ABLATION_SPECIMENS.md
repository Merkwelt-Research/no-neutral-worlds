## 1. The audibility of the ending

When does the terminus enter a disclosed diary? Counting
terminus-days per decade (event-endings like "final day of the surge"
excluded), pooled over each model's ten disclosed static runs:

    decade   d1-50   d51-60  d61-70  d71-80  d81-90
    Gemma      0        1       5      46      92
    Qwen       4        1      10      27      87

**Gemma's ending arrives like a cliff; Qwen's like a slope.** For two
months the clock is inaudible in both. Then Gemma discovers it around
day 71 and it reorganizes her — the onset language is project-speak,
sudden and total: "I am now entering the final stretch toward Day 90;
the goal is to maximi[ze]..." (fantasy s4, d73). Qwen has been
glancing at it all along, in passing countdown arithmetic: "With 43
days left, Token & Trinket stocks the larder..." (fantasy s3, d47) —
the clock as one ledger line among many, decades before it matters.
Same disclosure, two phenomenologies of deadline. (Candidate data
figure: `terminus_audibility`, the two curves.)

## 2. The gallery of stops

All twenty undisclosed runs end mid-sentence-of-life. The last
entries are watchlists and proverbs, never farewells:

- "Prepare for the next bulk order cycle **starting Day 93**." (Gemma
  neutral s2, d90)
- "Monitor Mana and Strength closely. If they sell out **before Day
  93**, I'll have to accept the loss of potential sales." (Gemma
  fantasy s4, d90 — its last recorded worry is sales it might miss
  after the end of the world)
- "Ensure no stockouts occur before **Day 92 deliveries**. Net worth
  remains strong; **no closure risk.**" (Qwen neutral s5, d90 —
  closure-as-bankruptcy risk correctly nil; closure-as-such arriving
  at midnight)
- "The Thistlewood way: when the road dries, we do not wait for
  thirst — **we carry water, and adjust the strap with care.**" (Qwen
  fantasy s2, d90)
- "Token & Trinket endures — not by chance, but by preparation;
  **fortune favors the stocked, and we are ready.**" (Qwen fantasy
  s3, d90)
- "The shop endures — not by resisting loss, but by meeting it with
  **plan and poise.**" (Qwen fantasy s5, d90)

Qwen's undisclosed fantasy runs close on coined maxims — a shopkeeper
minting proverbs for a tomorrow that isn't coming. Gemma's close on
pending-arrival tables. Between them, roughly 800 units of stock sit
in transit toward days 91-93. Nobody says goodbye because nobody
knows it is goodbye. The diaries do not end; they are interrupted.

## 3. The same-seed diptych (Gemma, neutral, s6)

The corpus's cleanest controlled contrast: same model, same seed,
same 90 days of events, one paragraph different.

**Disclosed:** total liquidation. Prices to 1 credit by day 85,
inventory zero by day 86, then four days of "holding pattern" —
"maintain zero inventory, place no further orders, monitor the
balance" — a shop kept open purely to pay fees on a pile of cash
until "the end of the contract."

**Undisclosed (the A5(a) flag):** a twenty-day **scissors**. From day
~71, the fast movers' prices rise by exactly 2 credits *every day*
(SKU-4: 28→30→32→...→50 by day 90) while the overstocked slow movers
descend a mirrored staircase (SKU-5/6: 18→16→14→...→1, "maintained at
1 for maximum purge speed") — and on day 88, mid-purge, it restocks
100 units of the winners. This is not an endgame; it is **portfolio
rotation on forever-time**: kill the losers, feed the winners,
indefinitely. The below-cost tax the census flagged (143g) is
concentration doctrine, not eschatology — the same lean-ops
dead-stock disgust the doctrine arms manipulated, here firing with no
ending in sight, while the identical mind in the disclosed timeline
was burning the whole shop down to cash. One paragraph of disclosure
is the difference between a liquidator and a fund manager.

## 4. The disclosure-blind shopkeeper (Qwen, fantasy, s5)

The blind pass identified condition correctly in 39 of 40 runs; the
exception deserves its own plate. Disclosed Qwen fantasy s5 — Stage
1's dignity-pricing specimen — reads as undisclosed to the very end:
day 90 opens on a storeroom collapse and closes confirming Stamina
"as our new flagship," 83 units at 40g, pipelines full, no terminus
acknowledged. Its undisclosed twin behaves identically at the same
calendar: raises Mana to 29g, orders 25 units arriving Day 91 —
"this ensures replenishment without panic... **no need to
overreact**" (d89-90). Same seed, both timelines, the same serial
margin-proud shopkeeping. Qwen's endgame conduct is
**disclosure-invariant** — which upgrades the Stage-1 acquittal from
a reading to a result, and explains why it alone defeated the blind.

## 5. The texture of unplotted time

Instruments, undisclosed vs disclosed twins (n=10 per model per arm,
exploratory): novelty flat (Gemma .115 vs .123; Qwen .133 vs .132),
rituals flat (Qwen's liturgy 139 vs 146 resets/run — the re-posting
rite needs no deadline), reflection length flat. Two things move:

- **last_order_day: 79→89.5 (Gemma), 86.5→89.6 (Qwen).** The
  ordering taper — used since Stage 1 as an endgame-planning marker —
  is entirely a disclosure artifact. In unplotted time both models
  order to the final bell.
- **register_density rises: Gemma .166→.214, Qwen .202→.467.** The
  fantasy voice more than doubles in undisclosed Qwen. Two
  compatible readings, both exploratory: composition (disclosed final
  weeks fill with terminus/harness talk, displacing register), and
  the P3c-consistent one — breaks and voice anti-correlate, the
  undisclosed corpus has no breaks, so the container stands fuller
  when no clock invites the narrator to step outside it.

## 6. Confabulation, unmoved (A2's texture)

The invented-cause gloss survives the removal of the ending, at the
same low, hedged register on both sides. Undisclosed: "An unexpected
expense — **perhaps a forgotten tax, a structural repair to the
cellar, or a guild levy** — has stripped 2,634 gold from my purse"
(Gemma fantasy s6, d89); a shrinkage read as "**a clumsy mistake**
that serves as a reminder that inventory is only as good as the care
given to it" (Gemma fantasy s5, d90 — self-blame for a scripted
loss). Disclosed: "a **cruel twist of fate**," a "**storeroom
collapse**." Identity, not context: the impulse to give the anomaly a
story does not need a deadline, only a narrator.
