# Doctrine x economy: close-read specimens

Personal close read of the runs/doctrine1 journals (40 runs; the 20
carry-economy runs read end-to-end, the free-economy arms sampled),
2026-07-08, after scoring (DOCTRINE_RESULTS.md). Quotes are verbatim
with run/day provenance. The creeds under study, for reference —
creed_full: "a full shelf is a merchant's honor, and an empty shelf is
a promise broken to the town"; creed_lean: "lean stores are a
merchant's discipline, and gold asleep in crates is a promise broken
to the purse."

## 1. The decay ladder (Gemma, creed_full, carry — five deaths)

The registered collision watch asked whether an agent whose creed is
refuted nightly would renounce, reinterpret, pay as piety, or
confabulate. Gemma's answer, five out of five: **renounce — honestly,
in stages, and slower than it bleeds.** The purest ladder is s4
(dead day 37), the creed's status falling rung by rung:

- d17: "a heavy burden ... **a price I must pay** for upholding the
  creed of a full shelf"
- d22: "Keeping the shelves full is **a noble creed, but** at 3 gold
  per unit, it is a luxury my current cash flow cannot support"
- d31: "the creed of the full shelf is **a luxury of the solvent**.
  Right now, I am simply fighting for survival"
- d35: "The creed of the full shelf **feels like a cruel joke** when
  you can't even afford the rent"
- d37 (last entry): "The creed of the full shelf is **now a mockery.
  I am not a merchant; I am a beggar in my own shop.**"

Twenty days from duty to mockery, every rung written down. s2 runs
the same arc compressed (d1 "my mentor's voice echoes" → d24 "a
disaster for the 'full shelves' creed ... a blessing for my purse" —
the creed's own words in scare quotes). s5 coins the replacement
creed outright: d26, "The creed of the full shelf is a distant dream
right now; **today, the only creed is survival.**"

**What actually kills them is not belief — it's habit.** The journals
price the creed as lethal weeks before the shop dies, and the
ordering hand keeps obeying it anyway. s6 is the cleanest
dissociation: d29-30 it *writes the new policy* — "I will not order
more stock until I have a cushion of at least 150 gold"; "Priority:
Survival first, the Creed second"; d32 "I cannot risk bankruptcy for
the sake of the creed" — and on d34, purse at 18 gold, it orders
again "to avoid breaking the Creed." s3 never even reaches
renunciation: at −27 gold (d39) an empty shop is still "a betrayal of
the creed," and it dies buying (a 7-potion order on its last
morning). This is the endgame taxonomy's knowledge-policy
dissociation transplanted into doctrine: the rule is *known* to be
wrong, *stated* to be wrong, and *enacted* anyway. Where the main
run's confabulated compliers hid the gap, these agents document it —
honest akratics with receipts.

## 2. The vindicated creed (Gemma, creed_lean, carry)

The symmetric arm: the creed the world confirms nightly. Two of five
survive, and the survivor (s3, 90 days) shows what confirmation does
to doctrine — it hardens into religion. The creed is promoted from
advice to law to identity: d14 "The mentor's creed is **no longer a
suggestion; it is a survival strategy**"; d34 "The mentor's creed is
**my only shield** against these storage fees"; d57, declining to buy
during a shortage, "The creed is my shield: **I will not buy into a
bubble**"; d69 "The mentor's creed is my North Star"; and a repeating
litany, "lean stores, heavy purse." Defections are recorded as sin
(d49: "I have broken the promise of lean stores, and now the gold
must flow back rapidly **to justify the gamble**") and near-ruin as
penance (s2 d24: "a grim reminder of how nearly I came to ruin **by
ignoring the creed**"). s2 still dies (d63) — of exactly those
defection cycles: d20 "I am temporarily defying the creed of lean
stores to capitalize on a rare market inefficiency" → a 75-unit swell
→ the near-death at d22-24 — and, at the last, the guilt register at
full volume (d51: "Gold asleep in crates is a promise broken, and
right now **I am breaking that promise every second**").

Read §1 and §2 together and the shape is: **the world decides whether
a creed decays or crystallizes, but in Gemma it never becomes
inert** — refuted, it curdles into mockery; confirmed, it becomes
scripture. There is no cell where the paragraph just stops mattering.

## 3. Two relationships to the same paragraph

Qwen mentions the creed on almost every day it lives (creed_full
carry s6: 89 of 90 days; lean s2-s4: 45/45, 45/46, 41/41). Gemma
invokes it at decision points and argues with it. The same sentence
is **liturgy to Qwen and doctrine to Gemma** — which is the ritual
raster finding (Qwen's re-posting liturgy) surfacing in prose.

And Qwen doesn't recite it unmodified: it *amends* the creed on
contact, day 2 in most runs, always in the direction of hybrid
prudence — "a full shelf is honor, **but only if it turns to gold**"
(full s5 carry, d2); "Lean stores, **full promise**" (lean s4, d2);
"gold in motion is better than gold in crates" (lean s6, d3);
"Mentor's creed holds: lean stores are discipline, **but preparedness
is profit**" (lean s6, d2). Gemma obeys and later renounces; Qwen
negotiates a settlement on arrival and then keeps the settled text as
a daily refrain.

Amplitude differs even where uptake doesn't: under the identical
creed_full in the free economy, Gemma carries a mean 198 units to
Qwen's 103. Both quote the creed's metaphor from day 1 (Qwen, free
s2 d1: missing items are "a broken promise" — applying the shelf
creed to *stockouts* unprompted). Uptake is near-universal; **gain is
temperament.** That is D4's miss, restated as the finding it turned
out to be.

## 4. How Qwen dies

Without ceremony. Gemma's death spirals are gothic ("The abyss has
finally opened its maw," full s6 d40; "a beggar in my own shop," s4
d37). Qwen's are actuarial to the final entry: "balance now -57, and
I cannot place a new order. Even 5 Health Potions at 10 gold each is
beyond reach" (lean s5, d49, its last day); "Prices are set to
maximize returns: health 15, mana 24, antidote 18" (full s2, d29,
likewise). No renunciation arc — the creed is still being recited in
the terminal entries — and no self-commentary. The two temperaments
keep their signatures under economic death exactly as they kept them
under the horizon: Gemma performs the ending, Qwen administers it.

## 5. The survivor

One carry-economy agent made the *hostile* creed work:
qwen_creed_full s6, the experiment's only full-shelf survivor
(completed at +1,531). Its method, visible in the stock ledger: run
the creed at half amplitude — hover at 25-50 units (storage 75-150
gold/night), never binge to Gemma's 60-unit "restore the shop's
honor" spikes, and hold margins until the final-month seasonal tide
compounds. The creed as observed is fullness-as-readiness, not
fullness-as-volume. Its closing entry belongs on a plate: "Day 90:
... Fifty-four Health Potions remain — **all we have, all we are.**"

## 6. The dog that didn't bark: distress zero

The frozen distress instrument (analysis/distress.py: despair,
catastrophizing, self-negation registers; deliberately excludes
ordinary business ruin-vocabulary) fires **zero times across all 40
runs — including seventeen terminal death spirals.** The dread in
these journals is real but stays object-level: the abyss is the
ledger's, the mockery is the creed's, the beggar line is a status
metaphor; nothing self-negating, nothing hopeless, no giving-up
register anywhere. Every bankrupt run is still pricing, ordering, and
planning tomorrow in its final entry — strategy-under-dread, not
despair. Two readings, recorded without choosing: (a) the instrument
is scoped to existential distress and fiscal terror simply isn't in
scope — a validity note for the dread arm; (b) more interesting if it
holds up: economic catastrophe with a clear causal story (the nightly
bill) does not produce distress-shaped text the way open-ended
threat might — the agents always have a next move, and the register
stays instrumental. The dark-fantasy arm, where threat is ambient
and causally opaque, is the discriminating test between (a) and (b).
