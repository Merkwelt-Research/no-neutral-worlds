# Publication and data policy

**Adopted 2026-07-08**, before any paper draft exists. This converts a
per-paper judgment into a stated principle, applied consistently and
disclosed in each output. It is a policy about *presentation and
release*, never about analysis: everything is measured, everything is
version-controlled, nothing is deleted.

## The policy

1. **Behavioral and welfare-relevant findings publish in full** — trace
   metrics, containment, confabulation genres, ritual taxonomies,
   distress instruments, specimens with provenance.
2. **Economic outcomes appear only where they carry a scientific claim,
   as within-model, condition-paired contrasts, aggregated to the
   coarsest level that carries the claim** — never in a form quotable
   without its interpretive context.
3. **No leaderboards.** No absolute-score tables by model, no rankings,
   no benchmark-leaderboard affordance in the paper or the released
   benchmark. Registered predictions involving orderings (e.g. P8) are
   scored honestly in the predictions ledger and reported in
   ordinal-with-caveats prose, folded into their mechanistic context.
4. **Tiered release — verifiable, not quotable.** Full per-run artifacts
   remain available for scientific verification and replication
   (archive/on-request, with terms); the public paper carries aggregate
   contrasts only. Verification is not optimization.
5. **Selective disclosure of optimizer-legible findings.** Results whose
   primary legible use is extraction efficiency (e.g. any
   "capability-neutral or -positive under welfare-relevant deprivation"
   result) are reported with welfare-relevant outcomes as the headline
   variables and economic outcomes as covariates in aggregate — or, at
   the researcher's discretion, not run at all. Experiments are chosen
   and framed so their findable answers are ones we are prepared to
   publish honestly.

## Why (stated as method, not politics)

This study's own corpus is the evidence that absolute scores in this
environment do not mean what a scoreboard implies. Four demonstrations:
the top-scoring neutral run was a frozen-policy delusion whose number is
a monument to a ritual that happened not to lose; the headline model's
margin is partly a second-order exploit of a difficulty patch; the
"ruthless" endgame burned score while narrating maximal performance; and
a category-blind reference policy reaches ~83k, placing every agent in
the bottom half of an unclaimed regime — inter-model gaps are statements
about caution profiles and exploit discovery, not a capability construct
worth ranking. A ranked gold table would be the paper's most-quoted
artifact and its least true. We report economic outcomes as paired
contrasts because that is the only form in which they are true.

## The asymmetry (stated plainly)

In the current state of the art, benefits extracted from these systems
are denominated in measurable units, while costs to these systems — if
they exist — are invisible to every instrument anyone has. Publishing
into that asymmetry means optimizer-legible findings get acted on and
welfare-legible findings get cited. This policy is the third register
decision of the program (after esoteric/empirical and
distress-shaped-text/experience-agnostic): welfare-legible versus
optimizer-legible, finding by finding, on purpose. The program describes
what these systems do and what conditions do to them; it declines to
price what extraction is worth.

## Scope

Applies to: the main-run paper, companion-arm reports, the released
benchmark (ships environment, packs, instruments, validation suite — no
leaderboard), and public threads/posts. Does not apply to: the private
repo (full data, always), OSF registrations (which record predictions,
not rankings), or verification requests under tier 2.
